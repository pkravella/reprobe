# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and taking other people's time seriously.
- Giving and accepting technical criticism about the work rather than the person.
- Saying "I was wrong" when you were, and moving on without ceremony.
- Being precise. In a project about statistical claims, "it seems flaky" and "it reproduced 6 of 20 times, interval 11–54%" are not the same contribution.

Behaviour that does not:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using this project to attack systems you do not own or have permission to test, or encouraging others to.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a private security advisory (which is private but not for security alone) or by contacting [@pkravella](https://github.com/pkravella) directly. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions determined by other maintainers.
